## 2. Calibration audit

**Finding, not yet a verdict: stated-p calibration cannot be meaningfully assessed yet.** Every position entered this month (TRX, CAKE, ETH, ZEC, MORPHO, UNI, ONDO, JUP, ASTER, AAVE, XLM, and now JUP again) was either (a) cut at the staged-entry confirmation checkpoint before ever reaching full size, (b) trimmed/exited on a discretionary conviction call ahead of invalidation, or (c) trimmed/exited on a sector-cap compliance correction. **None has been given the full 2-8 week hold window to resolve cleanly to target or invalidation** — the system's own confirmation/exit machinery is intervening before the probabilistic bet (what stated p is actually measuring) is ever fully tested. Stated p values clustered tightly in the 0.38-0.48 range (Tier C almost throughout, one Tier B at ZEC p=0.45) — too narrow a band and too few full-resolution outcomes (zero) to bucket meaningfully. **Flagging for next month:** track separately "legs that reached target/invalidation" vs. "legs cut/trimmed by rule" — only the former is real calibration data.

## 4. Shadow-book comparison

**Selection skill:** not yet meaningfully gradable. Only one confirmed-candidate rejection occurred all month (AAVE lost the 2026-08-26 AM EV tie-break to ASTER/MORPHO/JUP) and it converted to a real position that same PM checkpoint when a slot freed — no sustained forgone-alternative comparison exists. Every other confirmed candidate this month either had no competing candidate for its slot or was the only one to confirm. **Verdict: selection skill is untested so far, not validated or invalidated — the book simply hasn't yet faced a real "pick A over B, watch B outperform" scenario.**

**Exit skill: this is where the real signal is, and it is genuinely mixed.** Two weekly shadow-book refreshes on exited-position virtual continuations:
- **2026-08-24 refresh** (TRX/CAKE/ETH/ZEC/MORPHO/UNI, cut 08-16 to 08-22): realized -$31.23 vs. virtual-if-held +$1,094.37 — **opportunity cost -$1,125.60**, heavily weighted by the CAKE/ZEC/ETH RSI-rvol-driven false-negative cuts riding a sustained melt-up (F&G 46→73 over the window).
- **2026-08-31 refresh** (JUP/ASTER/ONDO/AAVE, cut 08-26 to 08-29): realized -$91.80 vs. virtual-if-held -$113.34 — **opportunity cost -$21.54** (i.e. cutting was mildly *better*), the first negative reading since tracking began, coinciding with the regime cooling (F&G 73→62).

**Net verdict:** the exit machinery — especially the staged-entry non-confirmation cut — cost real, quantified money during the sustained August melt-up and performed roughly neutral-to-good once the rally cooled. This is not evidence the rules are wrong; it's evidence they are **asymmetric by design** (built to protect against downside) and that asymmetry has a real cost in strongly trending regimes. This is the single most actionable finding of month 1 — see Section 6 proposal #1.

## 6. Rulebook proposals (for explicit user sign-off — not applied)

Per Section 13.6, CLAUDE.md is never edited without explicit user approval. The following are proposed amendments, ranked by evidence strength, drawn from LESSONS.md hypotheses #1, #2, #8, #9, #11 and this month's parameter scorecard/shadow-book findings:

**Proposal 1 — "Soft non-confirmation" at the staged-entry confirmation checkpoint (strongest evidence).** When a staged-entry's confluence drop below 7/10 is driven *only* by the mean-reverting/regime-overlay parameters (p3 RSI, p4 rvol, p6 funding, p10 F&G) while price direction and OI direction (the parameters most directly tied to each thesis's own named test) both held or improved, hold the half instead of cutting and revisit at the next checkpoint — rather than an automatic cut. **Evidence:** CAKE (+$11.96 realized vs. +$79.04 virtual-if-held), UNI (+$5.15 vs. an improving OI/price picture at cut time), and partially ZEC/ETH — 3-4 of the 4-7 staged cuts this month fit this exact pattern, and the aggregate shadow-book cost during the melt-up window was -$1,125.60. **Counter-evidence to weigh:** the 08-31 shadow refresh showed a near-neutral (-$21.54) reading once the regime cooled — this proposal would have helped during the melt-up but its value in a choppier or declining regime is untested. Recommend a trial period with explicit tracking rather than a permanent change.

**Proposal 2 — Named thesis-test breach trigger.** Add an explicit rule: 3 consecutive checkpoints of an entry thesis's own stated confirmation metric (almost always OI Δ so far) moving the wrong direction mandates at least a trim, independent of the general Bearish-count gate. **Evidence:** TRX (OI inverted and stayed inverted for 7 of 8 checkpoints before the eventual exit — the post-mortem itself judged the exit "arguably late, not early"), ONDO (same pattern, OI was the pillar that broke), and MORPHO's early cut (OI reversed within a single checkpoint, the most extreme version). This targets p7 OI specifically, the single highest-variance parameter identified in Section 3's scorecard.

**Proposal 3 — Post-trim/exit sector-exposure recheck.** Add an explicit Section 5 step: after any trim, exit, or resize (not just new adds/entries) with 2+ positions remaining in any sector, recompute each held sector's %-of-deployed-capital and correct immediately if any sector now exceeds 50%. **Evidence:** this exact failure mode fired once already (ONDO's 08-28 PM trim mechanically pushed DeFi Lending over its cap by shrinking the denominator, unrelated to DeFi Lending itself) and was only caught because the sector math happened to be checked while writing that report — there is currently no formal step requiring the check on the trim/exit path, only on the add path.

**Not proposed as a rule change (code/data-quality fix instead):** LESSONS.md #5 (sub-cent DMA serialization bug affecting SHIB/PEPE-class tokens in `scripts/parameters.py`) is a data pipeline bug, not a rulebook question — flagging for a code fix outside this review's scope, not a CLAUDE.md amendment.
